01 Professional Profile

Dr. Jie Li is a speech AI researcher and engineering leader with over 15 years of experience in automatic speech recognition (ASR), speech synthesis (TTS), and spoken-language large models. He currently leads the Speech Technology R&D Center at Xingchen AGI Lab, China Telecom Artificial Intelligence Technology Co. Ltd, where he serves as technical lead of the Xing-Audio Foundation Model — a fully self-developed speech intelligence stack serving nearly 500 million API calls per month across national telecommunications, public services, and consumer products.

Beyond algorithm research, his work is fundamentally about operating AI infrastructure at scale: maintaining production AI services under rigorous real-time-factor (RTF) and availability targets, orchestrating multi-instance deployments across domestic AI chips (Huawei Ascend, Denglin, Moore Threads), and delivering both public-cloud and private (on-premise) deployments in which customer data and models remain under customer control.

His work centers on making advanced speech intelligence accessible in every Chinese language variety — including 60+ regional dialects and low-resource minority languages such as Uyghur and Tibetan — and on building sovereign, standards-based AI infrastructure. He is the principal drafter of China's national AI standard for speech large models and leads national-level research programs on multimodal foundation models.

07 Relevance to Open Compute Governance

Dr. Li's perspective on open compute is grounded in the day-to-day reality of operating sovereign, standards-based AI infrastructure at national scale — and in extending such capability to underserved language communities. His experience maps directly onto the Working Group's core questions:

Operational perspective (Q13 · Compute provision and operations)

Production AI at national scale

Operates a production AI platform serving ~500M monthly calls under rigorous service-level discipline (RTF monitoring, multi-instance deployment, high-availability engineering) — the "implementer's view" the ToR explicitly seeks: what governance commitments are actually feasible for operators acting within their own legal and commercial environments.

Data & model protection (TF1 · Data immunity and control)

Confidentiality in practice

Manages speech pipelines handling sensitive user voice data for telecom and public-sector clients, including private (on-premise) deployments where customer data and models must remain entirely under customer control. Direct operational experience with the confidentiality, integrity and residency questions at the heart of the data immunity protocol.

Interoperability (TF5 · Technical interoperability)

Standards-based portability

As principal drafter of the national speech-LLM standard (GB/Z 45288.7), works on exactly the kind of interfaces, metadata and assurance artefacts that make workloads, models and results portable across facilities without lock-in — the core concern of TF5, applied to the AI-model layer of the compute stack.

Global South relevance (TF4 · Sustainability and enablement)

Digital inclusion through technology

Long-standing practice bringing advanced AI to low-resource language communities (Uyghur, Tibetan, 60+ dialects) in western China — first-hand understanding of how compute, models and skills reach regions without their own AI infrastructure, and how talent development sustains that reach over time.
